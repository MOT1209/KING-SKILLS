# hvac-short-video

Write hooks, scripts, captions and content plans for Arabic short videos about heating, ventilation and air conditioning (Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts).

## What the skill does

- Builds a hook for the first three seconds that fits the target short-video platform.
- Writes a full script with scenes, suggested shots and voice-over copy.
- Adds a publish-ready caption and platform-appropriate hashtags.
- Proposes content-series ideas and a monthly publishing calendar.
- Accounts for differences between Arabic dialects and common HVAC terminology.

## When to use

Use it when the user asks for a short video, script, hook, content idea, caption or publishing plan in the HVAC field, such as maintenance, fault diagnosis, energy saving or choosing the right unit.

Do not use it to produce or edit the video itself; use `arabic-shorts` instead. Do not use it for content outside HVAC.

## Example requests

- "اكتب لي سكربت ريلز عن تنظيف فلاتر المكيف." (Write a Reels script about cleaning AC filters.)
- "أفكار 30 فيديو قصير عن توفير استهلاك الكهرباء." (Ideas for 30 short videos about saving electricity.)
- "هوك وثلاث لقطات لفيديو عن صوت التكييف العالي." (A hook and three shots for a video about a loud AC.)

## What to expect

| Output | Details |
|---|---|
| Hook | A short, attention-grabbing opening line |
| Script | Scenes, shots and voice-over copy |
| Caption and publishing | A caption and hashtags suited to the platform |
| Content plan | Series ideas and a monthly publishing calendar |

## Requirements

No software tools. Audience, platform and target Arabic dialect are enough.
