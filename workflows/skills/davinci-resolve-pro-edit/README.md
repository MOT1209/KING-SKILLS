# davinci-resolve-pro-edit

Professionally edit a long-form YouTube recording in DaVinci Resolve, from a clean foundation cut to a finished, graphics-rich timeline.

## What the skill does

- Reuses or makes a clean foundation cut, removing bad takes, retakes and filler.
- Gives the first 30–45 seconds a short-form hook: screen zooms with highlights, punch-ins, luxury-tech graphics, a head pop-out, keyword pops, music that fades after the hook and a riser.
- Keeps the body moving with B-roll wherever the speaker explains or cites something: real articles and pages with the cited line marked, explainer graphics, logos and the speaker's own videos.
- Adds library sound effects without music through the body.
- Builds a new Pro Edit timeline with every addition on its own track.
- Produces chapters, an SRT and publishing copy.

## When to use

Use it whenever the user wants a long-form video or Resolve timeline professionally edited, polished or finished, or given zooms, graphics, B-roll, highlights, sound design or a hook — even without naming a skill.

Do not use it for shorts (use `create-video-shorts`) or for a clean cut with no graphics (use `davinci-resolve-video-edit`).

## Example requests

- "Edit this 40-minute tutorial in Resolve and add B-roll and a strong hook."
- "Polish my podcast recording with graphics and sound design."
- "Build a Pro Edit timeline with chapters and an SRT."

## What to expect

| Output | Details |
|---|---|
| Foundation cut | A clean, well-paced base timeline |
| Pro Edit timeline | Graphics, B-roll, zooms and sound effects on separate tracks |
| Delivery assets | Chapters, SRT and publishing copy |
| Verification | A checked timeline and a list of what changed |

## Requirements

DaVinci Resolve with the `davinci-resolve` MCP connected, the source recording, any supplied B-roll or logos, plus `ffmpeg` and Python for asset preparation.
