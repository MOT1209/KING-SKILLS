# feature-spec — feature specification

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Turns a feature idea into clear specifications: the problem, the scope, testable acceptance criteria, edge cases and open questions.

## When to use it
- You have a feature idea without details and want clarity before building.
- You want acceptance criteria the team agrees on before implementation.

## Example requests
1. "Write the specification for a password-reset-by-email feature in our app."
2. "I have an idea for an in-app notification system, turn it into specifications and identify the decisions I am missing."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| The specification | A problem, scope, numbered testable acceptance criteria and edge cases |
| Missing information | Open questions tagged as blocking or non-blocking |
| Limits | No detailed technical solutions and no invented numbers |
