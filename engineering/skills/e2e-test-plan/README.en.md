# e2e-test-plan — end-to-end test plan

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Drafts E2E scenarios for critical flows with priority, data and environment, and decides what deserves automation.

## When to use it
- Before launching a critical flow such as payment or booking.
- You are deciding what deserves automation and what to leave to a lower level.

## Example requests
1. "Write an E2E plan for the checkout flow in our store: cart, address, payment, confirmation."
2. "Which scenarios should I automate before launching the new sign-in system?"

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| The scenarios | A table with priority, steps, expected result and data |
| The environment | Accounts, simulators and cleanup requirements |
| Limits | No production data and no invented coverage numbers |
