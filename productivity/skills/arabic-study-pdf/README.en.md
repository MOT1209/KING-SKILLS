# arabic-study-pdf — Study PDF from lesson photos

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
It reads your lesson photos, asks a few short questions to learn what you need, then researches sources and designs a tidy Arabic PDF: a simplified explanation with German or English terms, a summary, practice questions with solutions, and a glossary.

## When to use it
- You have an exam and did not understand the lesson.
- You want a quick review and practice after the explanation, in one printable file.
- Do not use it for sourced research without a file (`research-summary`) or for slide decks (`presentations`).

## Example requests
1. "These are photos of a [subject] lesson, my exam is on [date] and I did not understand [topic]; make me a file to study from."
2. "Turn my note photos into an Arabic PDF with the German terms and 15 practice questions with solutions."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Before starting | A short round of questions with ready suggestions, so "yes" is enough |
| The file | Cover, contents, explanation, summary, questions, solutions on a separate page, glossary, sources |
| What is missing | Unreadable parts of the photos are reported to you instead of guessed |
| Limits | No invented sources; your photos are the reference if an outside source contradicts them |
