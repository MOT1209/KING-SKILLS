# research-summary — research and summary

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Searches several sources, actually reads and compares them, and then presents a clear summary with references and links so you can verify it yourself.

## When to use it
- You want to understand a new topic quickly using reliable sources.
- You want to compare opinions, studies or products.
- You need a documented summary for a report or a decision.

## Example requests
1. "Research the effects of remote work on productivity, and compare at least three sources, with links."
2. "Summarize the differences between the PostgreSQL and MySQL databases from the official documentation and tell me what is disputed."
3. "What is new on [topic] over the last two years? List the sources and their dates."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| The answer | Direct at the start, followed by the main findings linked to their sources |
| The comparison | Points of agreement and disagreement and uncertainties |
| References | Links that were actually opened and read |
| When it is not possible | An explicit notice instead of undocumented information |
