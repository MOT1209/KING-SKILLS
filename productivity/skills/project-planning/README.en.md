# project-planning — project planning

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Turns your goal into a practical plan: phases, tasks, dates, risks and success criteria.

## When to use it
- You have an idea or goal and do not know where to start.
- You want a realistic schedule with clear milestones.
- You want to anticipate risks before they occur.

## Example requests
1. "Plan the launch of a small online store within 8 weeks, with a team of three people."
2. "Break down a one-day professional meetup project into phases and tasks, and identify the main risks."
3. "I have two weeks to deliver an annual report. Arrange the tasks day by day."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Summary | The goal, the success criteria and what is out of scope |
| The plan | A table of phases, tasks, durations and dependencies |
| Risks | A table with likelihood, impact and a mitigation plan |
| Estimates | Marked as assumptions you can adjust; no invented budgets |
