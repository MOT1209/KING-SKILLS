# logo-concepts — logo directions

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Proposes three distinct visual directions for your logo, and explains for each why it suits your brand name and values and how it works in Arabic and English.

## When to use it
- At the start of logo design, before the details.
- When you are torn between a wordmark and a symbol.

## Example requests
1. "A brand called «سَنَد» for psychological support services, values: safety, warmth and privacy. Give me three logo directions."
2. "A company named Nova Logistics with the Arabic name «نوفا للشحن». Suggest logo directions that work in Arabic and Latin."
3. "I renamed my company and want to know whether to keep a wordmark or move to a symbol. Compare the directions for me."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Directions | Three distinct directions, each with a description, a reason and a use |
| Both languages | How the Arabic and Latin names are arranged |
| Tests | Scaling down, one color, light and dark backgrounds |
| Limits | Not final files and no guarantee of uniqueness or registration |
