# social-graphics — social media graphics

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Designs posts, stories and carousels for you at the right size for each platform, with short text and a consistent brand identity.

## When to use it
- You want a unified template for your posts.
- You are preparing a campaign on more than one platform.

## Example requests
1. "Design a 4:5 Instagram post template for a coffee brand, with a short headline and a logo in the corner, in Arabic."
2. "Turn this post into a vertical story, and check the safe zones."
3. "Prepare the specifications for a 5-slide carousel announcing a new product in Arabic."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Specifications | The platform ratio, the grid and the safe zones |
| Composition | A diagram showing where the elements go |
| Export | Format, size and alternative text |
| Limits | Verify the official sizes and the rights |
