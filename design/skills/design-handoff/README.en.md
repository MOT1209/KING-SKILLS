# design-handoff — design handoff

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Documents your design for developers with exact values for sizes, spacing, colors and fonts, with fixed component names and interaction states.

## When to use it
- Before the developer starts implementation.
- When the output differs from the design and you want a clear reference.

## Example requests
1. "Document the app's primary button: the sizes, spacing, colors and all its states. These are the design specifications: [values]"
2. "Convert my color palette and type scale into Design Tokens in JSON."
3. "Write a checklist for the developer to review the sign-up screen before closing the task."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Tokens | A table or JSON with names and values |
| Components | Complete sizes, spacing and states |
| RTL and assets | Mirroring notes and asset formats |
| Limits | Every number from your design, and gaps marked [TBD] |
