# design-brief — design brief

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Turns your vague idea into a one-page design brief: the goal, audience, message, tone, constraints and success criteria.

## When to use it
- Before you or your designer start any design work.
- When you feel your request is "unclear" or the team disagrees about it.

## Example requests
1. "I want an identity for a new coffee shop in Riyadh, help me write the design brief."
2. "I have a bilingual medical appointment booking app, turn my idea into a brief to hand to the designer."
3. "I have a children's clothing store and want an Eid campaign, help me write the design brief before I talk to the designer."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| The brief | One page with the defined sections |
| Questions | Up to 5 questions before the brief if something essential is missing |
| Missing information | Marked [TBD] with written assumptions |
| Limits | No invented market data and no detailed visual solutions |
