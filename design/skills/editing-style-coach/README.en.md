# editing-style-coach — Editing style coach

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Teaches you the tech-YouTube editing style you liked (consistent identity, big titles, picture-in-picture, screen-recording zooms, smooth motion) through concepts that carry over from one editor to another, with a learning plan from stage 0 to 7.

## When to use it
- You want to copy the style of reference videos and learn it step by step.
- You saw an effect and want its name and how to make it in CapCut, DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro.
- You need a title template or a checklist before editing a new video.

Do not use when: you want the cut actually made in Resolve (`davinci-resolve-video-edit`) or a fully executed edit (`davinci-resolve-pro-edit`).

## Example requests
1. "I like the style of these videos [links or screenshots]; make me a plan to learn it in 4 hours a week."
2. "How do I zoom into the screen with smooth motion in DaVinci Resolve?"
3. "Design a consistent title template for my channel, and review my latest video against the references."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Reference analysis | What was actually observed in the videos, with anything unseen marked |
| Effect breakdown | What changes (size, position, opacity, speed, color) and its name in your editor |
| Learning plan | Eight stages (0 to 7), one exercise each, ending in a short clip |
| Limits | It does not do the edit for you or promise views; menu names are checked against your version |
