# ux-review — UX review

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Follows the user's task step by step, identifies where they hesitate or get confused, and orders the findings by impact with a proposed fix for each point.

## When to use it
- You see users leaving the flow midway.
- Before launching a new version of an important flow.

## Example requests
1. "Review the sign-up flow in my app (screenshots attached) from opening the app to confirming the email."
2. "Why do customers call support after booking an appointment? These are the booking steps: [description]"
3. "Users leave the subscription form at the card field, review the form and tell me the likely reasons."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| The trace | The steps and the points of hesitation and confusion |
| Findings | Ordered by impact with a fix and a way to verify |
| What works | A list of the good |
| Limits | An expert judgment, not a measurement, and no invented figures |
