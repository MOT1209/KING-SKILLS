# writing-arabic — Arabic writing

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Helps you write new Arabic content (articles, letters, posts) or edit an existing text, with proofreading (spelling, grammar, punctuation) and improved style and clarity.

## When to use it
- You have an idea and want a finished article or post.
- You have a draft and want it polished and corrected.
- You want a formal or professional letter in a suitable tone.

## Example requests
1. "Write me a short social media post about the importance of daily reading, in a friendly tone, around 80 words."
2. "Proofread this text and improve its style while keeping its meaning, then tell me the main changes: [paste text]"
3. "Cut this paragraph in half without losing its main idea: [paste paragraph]"

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| New writing | Correct Arabic text in a suitable tone and length |
| Editing | The corrected text with a short list of changes and reasons |
| Missing information | Anything unavailable is marked [TBD] |
| Limits | No invented figures, quotations or sources |
