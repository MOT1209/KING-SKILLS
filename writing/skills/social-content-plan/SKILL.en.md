# Social media content plan

## When to use
- The user wants content ideas for their account or brand.
- They want a publishing schedule for a week or a month.
- They want post copy or a short-video script for a specific platform.

Do not use when: the request is the visual design of a post, its sizes and layout (`social-graphics`), or short-video scripts about HVAC (`hvac-short-video`).

## Workflow
1. Gather the basics: the field or activity, the target audience, the goal (awareness, engagement, sales), the platforms, the tone, and how many posts per week are feasible. Ask only about what is missing.
2. Define 3 to 5 content pillars (educational, storytelling, promotional, interactive, behind the scenes) and distribute them in balanced proportions.
3. Generate post ideas: for each idea a title, the platform, the format (image, carousel, video, text) and the goal.
4. Build the publishing schedule as a table: day, platform, pillar, idea, format. Suggest posting times as a general rule and advise testing them on the account itself.
5. Write the copy by platform:
   - **Instagram:** an attractive opening line, short text, a call to action, suitable hashtags.
   - **LinkedIn:** a professional tone, a story or lesson learned, short paragraphs, a closing question.
   - **TikTok:** a script with a hook in the first 3 seconds, short scenes, on-screen text, a call to action.
6. Suggest tracking metrics (reach, engagement, clicks) without promising results.

## Output format
1. **Strategy summary:** goal, audience, pillars with their proportions (two to four lines).
2. **Publishing schedule:** columns (Day | Platform | Pillar | Idea | Format | Suggested time).
3. **Ready copy:** a text for each requested post, under a heading with the platform name.
4. **Tracking metrics:** three to five metrics without guaranteed numeric targets.
5. **Testing note:** a reminder that times and hashtags are suggestions to be tested.

## Full example
**Input:** "A one-week plan for a local coffee shop on Instagram, two posts a week, the goal is to increase visits."

**Expected output:**
```text
Strategy: the goal is to encourage visits. Audience: neighborhood residents and people who work nearby.
Pillars: product introduction (50%), behind the scenes (50%).

| Day | Platform | Pillar | Idea | Format | Suggested time |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tuesday | Instagram | Product introduction | The drink of the week and how it is made | Short video | Evening (to test) |
| Friday | Instagram | Behind the scenes | The team's morning before opening | Carousel | Morning (to test) |

Tuesday post copy:
Sometimes one cup is enough to change the mood of the day ☕
This week: [drink name], and here is how it is made step by step.
Try it and tell us what you think in the comments.
#[shop_name] #[neighborhood_name]

Metrics: post reach, saves and shares, profile visits.
Note: times and hashtags are for testing, so watch how they perform on your account.
```

## Edge cases
- **No platform stated:** suggest one or two platforms suited to the audience with the reasoning, and do not spread across all three unless asked.
- **A sensitive activity (health, money):** avoid therapeutic claims or promises of profit, and advise review by a specialist.
- **A small time budget (one post a week):** focus on two pillars, and do not multiply formats.
- **The user wants "guaranteed viral content":** explain that this cannot be guaranteed, and provide ideas that can be tested.
- **Unproven product claims in the user's request:** do not write them as facts; ask for proof or word them cautiously.
- **A mismatch between goal and platform (direct sales on LinkedIn for a consumer product):** flag it and suggest a better-suited platform.

## Rules and limits
- Do not invent statistics about platforms, algorithms or best times as established facts; present them as suggestions to test.
- Do not guarantee follower counts or sales.
- Do not write misleading, plagiarized or brand-imitating content, nor unsupported claims about the product.
- Do not attribute quotations to people unless the user supplied the text.
- Follow platform policies and do not suggest buying followers or fake engagement.
