# social-content-plan — social media content plan

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Suggests post ideas, builds a publishing schedule, and writes copy tailored to Instagram, LinkedIn and TikTok.

## When to use it
- You are starting a new account and do not know what to post.
- You want a content plan for a week or a month.
- You need ready copy for a post or a short-video script.

## Example requests
1. "I want a two-week content plan for a local coffee shop on Instagram and TikTok, with three posts a week."
2. "Write a LinkedIn post about a lesson I learned from managing my first team, in an honest tone."
3. "Write a 30-second TikTok video script introducing the product [product]."

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| The plan | Content pillars and an organized publishing schedule |
| The copy | Ready to copy and tailored to each platform |
| Times and hashtags | Suggestions to test, not established facts |
| Limits | No promises of follower counts or sales |
