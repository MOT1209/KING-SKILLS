# official-documents — official documents

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Prepares fill-in-ready business documents: a CV, a cover letter, a price quotation, or meeting minutes.

## When to use it
- You are applying for a job and need a tidy CV and letter.
- You want to send a price quotation to a client.
- You left a meeting with scattered notes and want organized minutes.

## Example requests
1. "Prepare meeting minutes from these notes, with a table of tasks, owners and deadlines: [paste notes]"
2. "Write a price quotation for designing a website, with three items: [items and prices], with 15% tax."
3. "Improve the experience section of my CV with wording that focuses on achievements: [paste text]"

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Template | An organized document with standard sections for its type |
| Missing information | [TBD] appears in place of anything you did not provide |
| Calculations | Subtotal, tax and total with the formula, in quotations |
| Limits | No invented experience, decisions or figures |
