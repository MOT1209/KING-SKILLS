# translation-arabic-english — Arabic ⇄ English translation

> English translation of [README.md](README.md).

## What the skill does
Translates text between Arabic and English accurately and naturally, unifies recurring terminology, and preserves the tone and formatting of the original.

## When to use it
- You are translating an article, a contract, a marketing pitch or a letter.
- You have a translation and want it reviewed.
- You want consistent terminology across a long document.

## Example requests
1. "Translate this marketing paragraph into Arabic in an enthusiastic tone, without literal translation: [text]"
2. "Translate this technical report into English, using the following term glossary: [glossary]"
3. "Review my English translation of this letter and tell me what sounds unnatural: [original text] [my translation]"

## What to expect
| Item | What you get |
|---|---|
| Translation | Faithful to the meaning, in the formatting of the original |
| Notes | Explanation of ambiguous wording and alternatives |
| Terminology | A unified table for long texts |
| Legal and medical | A notice to have a certified translator review it |
